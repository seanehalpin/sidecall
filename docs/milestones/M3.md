## Spec changes (call-outs)

- **§6.10 is new:** voice turns, the pipeline, pick binding constants, the announcer, and measured latency.
- **Spoken turns skip the narration hold.** Holding text cost up to several seconds whenever the talker read files first. Instead the talker may open with a short acknowledgement ("Sure, one sec") before reading code.
- **The TalkerProcessor groups sentences itself.** Pipecat's aggregator held short openers until more text arrived, which pushed first audio from 1.2 s to 3.8 s.
- **Two STT timing fixes:** Parakeet put the last word about 0.35 s late, and segment starts drifted under load. Both would have misplaced `[n]` markers.
- **`call.page` is new**, so spoken turns carry the page and a screenshot like typed ones.
- **Notifications:** `notify.action` is "call" when no call is active; during a call, results are spoken instead.
- **§5.5:** the offscreen document, not the rail, checks the mic grant.

## Manual verification

Setup: restart the daemon (`cd daemon && uv sync && uv run sidecall start --preload-voice`), rebuild and reload the extension (`cd extension && pnpm build`, then the reload icon), and refresh your app's tab. Speakers are fine, since echo passed in Phase 1.

1. **Real conversation.** Start a call and say "what's on this page?" *Expected:* a short spoken answer about the page you're on, starting within about 1.5 s. No "show me the page".
2. **A change by voice.** Say "make the heading red". *Expected:* a one-sentence confirmation, "Task queued" in the panel, and later, after you've been quiet for a moment, a spoken "that's done" (or similar). The page updates.
3. **Point while talking.** Say "make this… match that" while ⌥-clicking one element on "this" and another on "that". *Expected:* the transcript shows `[1]` and `[2]` next to the right words, the chips dim, and the edit matches.
4. **Pause mid-sentence while pointing.** Say "make this", pause and pick something, then say "bigger". *Expected:* one turn, not two.
5. **Interrupt.** Ask for something long ("explain how this page is built") and talk over it. *Expected:* it stops quickly. Tell me whether it feels fast enough at the default setting.
6. **Announcement timing.** Queue a change, then keep talking about something else. *Expected:* the "done" announcement waits until you pause, and never cuts you off.
7. **Missed results.** Queue a change and hang up straight away. *Expected:* a "done — tap to talk" notification. Click it: the panel opens, a call starts, and the agent opens with a one-sentence summary.
8. **Typing during a call.** Type a message while on the call. *Expected:* it's answered in text and the call stays up.

## Known issues

- **Barge-in is about 55 ms over target** at the approved VAD setting (see Acceptance).
- **Latency is close to the 1.5 s limit** in the browser (about 1.4 s median). Sonnet as the talker would be slower; Haiku is the default for this reason.
- **Merged turns keep a full stop at the join** ("Make this [1]. Match…"), which is cosmetic.
- **Page capture timing:** the page is captured when the trigger fires (call start, tab change, start of an utterance), not when the turn is submitted, so a quick scroll just before you stop speaking isn't captured. A tab-change capture waits for the navigation to finish.
- **A cut-off spoken reply is recorded as complete** when typed text interrupts it during a call.
- **Turn thresholds** (continuation 1.5 s, 1 s hold after a pick) are first guesses tuned on synthetic speech. Your checks 3 and 4 tune them.
- **A real notification click** (check 7) isn't covered by automation.
